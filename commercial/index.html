Damage Prevention

Finding and Preventing Water Risks at Your Property

Owning commercial property comes with a duty of care, which means keeping the premises safe. Hidden moisture, aging plumbing, and inadequate waterproofing can result in sudden failures that cause injuries, property damage, and costly lawsuits. A Richland Restoration risk assessment looks at every part of the property for exposure to water intrusion, with moisture monitoring tools and thermal imaging doing the detection. Findings are delivered in writing, with risks identified, priorities assigned, and specific prevention measures recommended.

An assessment does two jobs: finding vulnerabilities protects people and the property, and the record of due diligence can protect you legally should something happen. Courts and insurance companies look favorably on property owners who maintain regular professional inspections and act on expert recommendations. Our recommendation is a moisture assessment once a year for every commercial property, and more frequent checks in high-risk spots like mechanical rooms, basements, and older plumbing systems.
